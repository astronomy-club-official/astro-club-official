International Competition

GAAC Global Challenge

The Global Astronomy & Astrophysics Challenge is an international competition organized by the STEM October Astronomy Club for high school students worldwide. Contributions fund the global qualification rounds, cloud examination servers, international merit medals, and travel sponsorships for top finalists competing across Astronomy, Astrophysics, Cosmology, Observational Astronomy, and Orbital Mechanics.

Academic Curriculum

Training Season (24+ Sessions)

Powers our structured 24-session annual academic curriculum covering Celestial Mechanics, Theoretical Astrophysics, Cosmology, and Observational Astronomy. Contributions directly subsidize Olympiad problem-solving seminars, study materials, and specialized computational software licenses for all student members.

Astrophotography & Observation

AstraVale Astrophotography

AstraVale is the premier astrophotography and stargazing workshop series hosted by the STEM October Astronomy Club. Contributions equip students to capture the cosmos through practical telescope handling, camera optics, narrowband filters, deep-sky celestial imaging, astronomical image processing, and overnight observation expeditions.

Need-Blind Inclusion

Need-Blind Student Sponsorship

Guarantees 100% need-blind participation across all club initiatives. Contributions cover competition registration fees, telescope access, laboratory safety gear, and travel subsidies, ensuring that financial barriers never prevent talented STEM students from pursuing astronomy and astrophysics.
